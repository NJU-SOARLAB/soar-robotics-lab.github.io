SOARLAB

Flying General Intelligence Lab

Flying robots, embodied agents, real systems. Led by Hao Xu, we build embodied and collaborative intelligence for aerial robots, humanoids, and emerging flying humanoid platforms, with a focus on robotics agents, world/action models, VLA-style robot foundation models, distributed perception, SLAM, and reinforcement learning.

PI

Principal Investigator

Hao Xu Tenure-track Associate Professor Personal homepage →
Nanjing UniversitySchool of Intelligent Science and Technology

Hao Xu received his Ph.D. from the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Shaojie Shen. He has worked as a multi-sensor information fusion algorithm engineer at DJI and as an associate researcher at Beihang University, and joins Nanjing University as a tenure-track associate professor. His prior work spans spatial intelligence for aerial and legged robots, aerial swarm state estimation, distributed SLAM, and autonomous swarm systems; his future research centers on embodied and collaborative intelligence.

Dr. Xu has published in major robotics journals and conferences including IEEE TRO, IEEE RAL, ICRA, and IROS, and serves as a reviewer for these venues. The collaborative work RACER received the IEEE Transactions on Robotics King-Sun Fu Memorial Best Paper Award, the first time this award went to a team from China. His released systems include D2SLAM, Omni-Swarm, VINS-Fisheye, and FoxTracker.

Research

Research Pillars

01

Robotics Agent

Agent-oriented perception, planning/control, world models, simulation, and behavior decision-making for robots operating under real hardware constraints.

02

WAM/VLA Robot Foundation Models

World-action and vision-language-action modeling for robot foundation models validated on aerial robots, humanoids, and flying humanoid platforms.

03

Distributed Perception & Swarms

Distributed SLAM, aerial swarm state estimation, reinforcement learning, and autonomous swarm systems, building on the lab's long-standing core robotics work.

News

All news →
  1. Oct 15, 2026 Paper
    New preprint: Spatial Memory by Agents, for Agents. Coding agents design a robot’s spatial memory by writing code, running benchmarks, and iterating on feedback. We call this Guided System Improvement (GSI). Across five task tracks, the best configuration improves the average unified score by about 51% over the strongest all-task baseline. Joint work with Mondo Robotics. arXiv · Project page
  2. Sep 30, 2026 Award
    Our collaborative paper AUG: Perception-Aware UAV Graph-Based Planning for GNSS-Denied Navigation in Feature-Sparse Environments received an Honorable Mention for the IROS 2026 Best Paper Award on Safety, Security, and Rescue Robotics.
  3. Sep 30, 2026 Paper
    ECHO-G is out: humanoid robots that gesture while they talk, landing each move on the beat. ECHO-G combines text and speech latents for semantically rich full-body gestures with end-to-end whole-body control, and runs on an embedded Jetson Orin AGX. Joint work with Mondo Robotics and the HKUST Aerial Robotics Group. arXiv · Code · Video
  4. Apr 27, 2026 Lab
    Early members will help build the lab’s infrastructure, including compute servers, experimental space, and UAV and robot validation platforms.
  5. Apr 27, 2026 Admissions
    Our most urgent directions are WAM/VLA robot foundation models and AI agents. Students with strong interests in distributed perception, SLAM, reinforcement learning, aerial swarms, and real robotic systems are also welcome. See the recruiting page.
