news

  1. Oct 15, 2026 Paper
    New preprint: Spatial Memory by Agents, for Agents. Coding agents design a robot’s spatial memory by writing code, running benchmarks, and iterating on feedback. We call this Guided System Improvement (GSI). Across five task tracks, the best configuration improves the average unified score by about 51% over the strongest all-task baseline. Joint work with Mondo Robotics. arXiv · Project page
  2. Sep 30, 2026 Award
    Our collaborative paper AUG: Perception-Aware UAV Graph-Based Planning for GNSS-Denied Navigation in Feature-Sparse Environments received an Honorable Mention for the IROS 2026 Best Paper Award on Safety, Security, and Rescue Robotics.
  3. Sep 30, 2026 Paper
    ECHO-G is out: humanoid robots that gesture while they talk, landing each move on the beat. ECHO-G combines text and speech latents for semantically rich full-body gestures with end-to-end whole-body control, and runs on an embedded Jetson Orin AGX. Joint work with Mondo Robotics and the HKUST Aerial Robotics Group. arXiv · Code · Video
