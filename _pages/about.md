<section class="soar-section soar-pillars">
  <header class="soar-section__head">
    <p class="soar-kicker">Research</p>
    <h2>研究主线</h2>
  </header>
  <div class="soar-pillar-grid">
    <div class="soar-pillar">
      <span class="soar-pillar__index">01</span>
      <h3>Robotics Agent</h3>
      <p>面向 Agent 的感知、规划控制、世界模型、仿真和行为决策，让机器人能够在真实硬件约束下理解环境、生成动作并持续学习。</p>
    </div>
    <div class="soar-pillar">
      <span class="soar-pillar__index">02</span>
      <h3>WAM/VLA 机器人基础模型</h3>
      <p>围绕 World-Action Model 与 Vision-Language-Action 建模，推进可在无人机、人形机器人和飞行人形机器人上验证的机器人基础模型。</p>
    </div>
    <div class="soar-pillar">
      <span class="soar-pillar__index">03</span>
      <h3>分布式感知与集群</h3>
      <p>分布式 SLAM、无人机集群状态估计、强化学习与自主集群系统，是课题组长期积累的经典机器人核心算法。</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
